Wellbeing Self-Check

Take a few minutes to think about how you have felt at work over the last two weeks.

There are 24 questions based on the four parts of Te Whare Tapa Whā. At the end you will see your wellbeing profile and some programs that might help.

This is just a reflection activity, not a medical or psychological test. It takes about 4 minutes.

Your wellbeing profile

Scores go from 1 to 10. A higher score means that area may need more attention.

These scores are only for personal reflection and are not clinically validated. If you need support, you could talk to your Employee Assistance Program. In Australia you can call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
